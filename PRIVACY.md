# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Reminder app ("the app") uses, what leaves your device, and why. Reminder is developed by Salahuddin Saiet.

## Summary

- No account, name, email or phone number is ever asked for.
- Your location is used on your device to work out the Qibla direction. It is never stored or sent anywhere.
- The app sends anonymous diagnostic logs so problems can be found and fixed.
- No data is sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you across other apps.

## Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app:

- Your selected area
- Your reminder settings (on/off, minutes before prayer, hidden prayers)
- Your theme and wallpaper choice
- Cached prayer times for months you have viewed
- A randomly generated device ID (see below)

## Location

When you open the Qibla screen, the app asks for permission to use your approximate location. It is used on your device to calculate the direction to the Kaaba and is not stored, logged or sent to any server. You can decline this permission; the rest of the app still works.

## Information sent from your device

### Prayer times

To show prayer times, the app requests the list of areas and the times for your selected area from our backend, hosted by [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy).

### Diagnostic logs

Release versions of the app send diagnostic logs to the same backend to help find and fix bugs, for example when prayer times fail to load or reminders fail to schedule. A log entry may include:

- A random device ID created by the app. It is not linked to your name, phone number, advertising ID or any hardware identifier.
- Your device's operating system and OS version
- The app version
- Your selected area and the month being loaded
- Reminder scheduling details, such as how many reminders were set
- Error messages

Logs do not include your location. They are used only for debugging and improving the app, and are kept only as long as they are useful for that.

### App updates

The app checks for updates through [Expo](https://expo.dev/privacy) (EAS Update). As with any network request, Expo receives standard technical information such as your IP address and app version in order to deliver updates.

## Notifications

If you turn on prayer reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification token is sent to any server.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices

- **Location:** You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.
- **Notifications:** You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.
- **Local data:** Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device.
- **Diagnostic logs:** You can ask for logs to be deleted by emailing the address below. Because logs are not linked to your identity, we may not be able to find entries from your device specifically.
